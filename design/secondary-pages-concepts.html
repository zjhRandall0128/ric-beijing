About RIC Beijing

Regional instrument support for WMO Region II

RIC Beijing provides calibration, intercomparison and technical support for meteorological instruments, supporting national observing systems through traceability, quality assurance and capacity development.

RIC

WMO RIC Designation

Designated to support calibration and measurement traceability for meteorological instruments.

CN

Based in Beijing

Serving regional partners with consultation, workshops, comparison and calibration services.
